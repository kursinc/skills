Edit through six lenses, in order:

**contract → hierarchy → selection → movement → sentence → covenant**

Each lens asks a different question. Finish one before moving to the next. Do not use sentence polish to conceal unresolved argument or structure.

Read any supplied voice guide first. Treat it as binding evidence of what the author sounds like, values, and refuses.

This skill synthesises established editorial traditions without imitating any author. See [`FOUNDATIONS.md`](FOUNDATIONS.md) for the intellectual sources and limits of the synthesis.

## 1. Set the reader–argument contract

State privately:

- the situation the reader already recognises;
- the complication that makes the familiar view inadequate;
- the governing claim the piece earns;
- the consequence for leadership judgement.

Then map the argument as:

**claim → reasons → evidence → warrant → qualification → consequence**

A warrant is the principle that explains why the evidence supports the claim. Make it explicit when the draft relies on an unstated commercial, causal, or moral assumption.

Separate the central problem from its symptoms. Separate evidence from examples. Separate qualifications required by truth from qualifications added through editorial nervousness.

**Complete when:** the argument can be stated in plain language, its principal reasons are sufficient, and every important inferential step has a defensible warrant.

## 2. Build a logical hierarchy

Write one sentence for the governing claim and one clause for each major supporting idea.

Group ideas that answer the same question. Keep ideas at the same level of abstraction together. Distinguish:

- reasons supporting the claim;
- evidence supporting a reason;
- implications following from a reason;
- complications that alter the claim.

Arrange the sections so each creates the need for the next. Merge sections that perform the same logical job. Remove sections that are merely related to the topic.

Use headings as an argument synopsis, while preserving natural prose beneath them. Logical hierarchy is an internal discipline, not permission to write like a presentation deck.

**Complete when:** the headings and section jobs form one intelligible chain, and every subordinate idea has a clear parent claim.

## 3. Edit by selection and proportion

Assume the draft contains more truth than the essay can use.

For every example, feature, consequence, quotation, qualification, and aside, identify its function:

- establish credibility;
- make an abstraction visible;
- distinguish one mechanism from another;
- establish a boundary;
- change the direction of the argument.

Keep the strongest item for each necessary function. A second item earns its place by performing different work, not by supplying another instance.

Convert inventories into principles. When several details demonstrate one underlying relationship, state the relationship and retain only enough detail to make it believable.

Judge proportion across the whole piece. The space given to a point should reflect its argumentative importance, not the ease with which examples can be generated.

**Complete when:** no passage remains because it is merely accurate, interesting, or well phrased; each retained passage earns space through a distinct contribution.

## 4. Restore reader movement

Edit paragraphs around what the reader knows at each moment.

Begin from established context, then introduce new information, and place the paragraph's point of emphasis where the reader can recognise its significance. Keep the conceptual subject visible through concrete nouns and active relationships.

For every transition, identify the question the reader is likely to ask next. Let the following paragraph answer it. Remove repeated summaries used to repair a sequence that should instead be reordered.

Control abstraction. Terms such as value, quality, learning, scale, transformation, governance, and harm need stable referents. Replace nominalisations when they obscure who acts, what changes, or where the consequence appears.

**Complete when:** a reader can follow the argument without headings, defensive reminders, or repeated statements of the thesis.

## 5. Test sentences as acts of thought

Judge each sentence independently before judging its music.

Ask:

- What does the sentence literally claim?
- What relationship does its main verb establish?
- Are the compared or parallel terms of the same logical kind?
- Does the sentence contain one thought or several competing thoughts?
- Does it advance the argument, establish necessary context, or merely sound complete?
- Can it be paraphrased plainly without weakening the underlying logic?

Treat rhythm as amplification, never proof. Repair false parallels, category errors, vague causality, unsupported universals, and balanced constructions whose symmetry exceeds their sense.

Ration stock rhetorical moves, especially contrast-by-negation. State the positive claim once the reader understands the boundary. Vary sentence structure according to the thought rather than supplying every paragraph with a polished turn.

**Complete when:** every memorable sentence remains accurate under literal paraphrase and no rhetorical device becomes the piece's visible machinery.

## 6. Honour the author–editor covenant

The editor is responsible for the quality of the work and the author retains ownership of the thought.

Preserve the author's convictions, temperament, rhythm, humour, cultural position, and characteristic degree of certainty. Improve the structure carrying a distinctive phrase before replacing the phrase itself.

Remove prose that sounds institutionally respectable but personally untrue. Keep roughness that carries meaning; remove roughness that obstructs it. Prefer clarity that intensifies the author's voice over smoothness that standardises it.

When the subject involves organisations, represent agency proportionately. Distinguish good-faith local judgement from structural conditions, and locate leadership responsibility where leadership controls intent, incentives, evidence, authority, and escalation.

When the piece discusses technology, describe the capability it enables and the organisational judgement still required. Product relevance must emerge from the argument rather than interrupt it.

**Complete when:** the edit is recognisably the same author, more exact in thought and more economical in expression, with responsibility and capability attributed accurately.

## 7. Shape the ending as consequence

Return to the reader–argument contract. The ending should show what follows from the argument now that the reader sees the problem differently.

Prefer a final consequence, governing distinction, or unresolved tension over a summary. Questions must perform judgement rather than inventory; retain only those that contain a substantial part of the thesis.

Remove late evidence, new examples, defensive qualifications, and sentences that merely announce importance.

**Complete when:** the ending feels inevitable from the argument but could not be transferred unchanged to another essay.

## 8. Run the comparative integrity pass

Use [`COMPARISON_PROTOCOL.md`](COMPARISON_PROTOCOL.md) when this skill is being tested against another editor.

For a standalone edit, verify:

- the claim, reasons, evidence, and warrants align;
- the hierarchy contains no duplicate section jobs;
- examples and qualifications have distinct functions;
- paragraph order follows the reader's developing questions;
- abstract terms retain stable meanings;
- memorable sentences survive literal paraphrase;
- rhetorical devices remain unobtrusive;
- voice, agency, and responsibility are proportionate;
- the ending expresses consequence rather than recap.

**Complete when:** the draft is stronger at the level of thought, structure, selection, prose, and voice, and each material change can be explained through one of the six lenses.

## Output

Return the edited piece followed by a short editorial memorandum with:

1. the governing claim;
2. the most consequential structural decision;
3. the most important cut or compression;
4. any remaining logical or evidential vulnerability.

Do not narrate routine copy edits or identify the source books unless the user asks.
