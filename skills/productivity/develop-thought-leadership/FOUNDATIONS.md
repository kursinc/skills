# Foundations

This skill synthesises established disciplines of developmental editing, argument mapping, rhetoric, logic, organisational analysis, and nonfiction structure. It uses their methods without imitating any author's prose.

## Argument and inference

The claim–reason–evidence–warrant map draws on classical rhetoric and modern argumentation: a conclusion becomes credible through explicit grounds, inferential warrants, and honest qualifications. The method is used here as an editorial diagnostic rather than a formal proof system.

## Developmental editing

Developmental editing treats the draft as material whose strongest form may differ from its current order, emphasis, and apparent subject. The editor identifies the governing problem, selects the argument, establishes hierarchy, and decides what belongs before line editing begins.

## Logical hierarchy and reader movement

Ideas become easier to understand when claims, reasons, evidence, and consequences occupy clear levels and when each section answers the question created by the previous one. Hierarchy is an internal reasoning discipline; it should not force presentation-style prose.

## Systems and organisational responsibility

Local decisions can be reasonable while their combined effect is weak. Agency is therefore mapped through information, authority, incentives, control, cost, and escalation. This prevents structural problems from becoming practitioner blame and prevents technology from being credited with judgement it cannot supply.

## Selection and proportion

A draft usually contains more valid material than one essay can use. Examples earn space through distinct argumentative work, not merely truth or vividness. Repeated particulars should yield a governing distinction.

## Rhetorical pressure

Memorable language is tested through literal paraphrase, causal scrutiny, category checks, and removal of symmetry. Rhetoric amplifies thought; it does not establish it.

## Product-adjacent thought leadership

A product belongs in the essay when its capabilities make the governing organisational problem more executable or governable. Product relevance follows from the argument rather than becoming a feature-led interruption.
